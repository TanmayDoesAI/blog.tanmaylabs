Hi, I'm Tanmay. Welcome to my personal blog.

My day job is in Generative AI, but this space is for everything else. This is my public notebook for tinkering, side projects, and whatever I'm currently trying to figure out in tech.

For the longest time, I waited to share projects until they were "perfect," which basically meant I never shared anything. This blog is my attempt to fix that. It's a place for the messy, in-between parts of building things, the process is more interesting than the polished result, anyway.

You can expect the topics here to be a bit random. It might be a walkthrough of a project I'm working on, notes on a new tool, or just thoughts on something I'm learning. The only real theme is sharing the process.

This whole thing is a learning experience, so if you see something interesting, spot a mistake, or just want to connect, I'd love to hear from you.
